How we work

Five stages, each with a defined output. You should never be unsure which stage you are at or what happens next.

  1. Consultation

    We map what you actually need to protect — assets, liabilities, people and income — and review any cover you already hold for gaps and overlap.

  2. Comparison

    We approach the insurers best suited to your risk and bring back comparable terms: premium, limits, excesses and the exclusions that matter.

  3. Guidance

    We talk you through the options and answer the awkward questions, so you decide knowing what is covered, what is not, and what any gap costs to close.

  4. Activation

    We place the cover, confirm it, and deliver your documents, schedule and certificates, with payment terms and warranties confirmed in writing.

  5. Support

    Endorsements, additions, queries and claims run through your named contact. We start the renewal review well before expiry so nothing lapses.

If we fall short

Raise it with your account contact first. If it is not resolved to your satisfaction, escalate in writing to the Principal Officer at info@goldoak.co.ke. Every complaint is logged, investigated and answered in writing. If you remain dissatisfied once our internal process is exhausted, you retain the right to refer the matter to the Insurance Regulatory Authority, and we will tell you how rather than obstruct it.

What we will ask you for

  • Individuals: national ID or passport, KRA PIN and contact details
  • Businesses: certificate of incorporation, CR12 and directors' identification
  • Motor: logbook, driver details and a valuation report where required
  • Property: a schedule of assets with values, and location details
  • Medical: a member list with dates of birth and any declared conditions
  • Group schemes: staff census, payroll band and current scheme documents
  • Renewals: your expiring policy schedule and claims history
