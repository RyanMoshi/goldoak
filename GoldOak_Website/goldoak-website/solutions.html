Solutions

Cover for individuals, families and businesses, arranged across several insurers so you see more than one set of terms.

Medical insurance

Inpatient, outpatient, maternity, dental and optical

Cover for individuals, families and staff schemes, placed with insurers we know settle claims. We set out the inpatient limit, the outpatient limit and the waiting periods in writing before you commit.

  • Inpatient cover for admission, surgery and specialist care
  • Outpatient consultations, diagnostics and prescriptions
  • Maternity, dental and optical benefits
  • Access to accredited hospitals nationwide
  • Last expense, evacuation and personal accident riders

Life assurance and education

Term, whole life, education and income protection

Protection for the people who depend on your income, and a disciplined way to fund school fees that keeps paying if you are no longer there.

  • Term life for the largest benefit per shilling
  • Whole life with a savings element
  • Education plans with waiver of premium
  • Income protection and credit life
  • Critical illness and disability riders

Motor insurance

Private, commercial, PSV and fleets

Comprehensive and third-party cover for personal and business vehicles, with fleet arrangements consolidated onto one renewal date.

  • Comprehensive, third party, and third party fire and theft
  • Commercial motor for own goods and general cartage
  • PSV cover including statutory passenger liability
  • Excess protector and political violence extensions
  • Driver and occupant personal accident

Property and general

Home, fire, theft, all risks and business interruption

Cover for the premises and assets a household or business depends on — and for the income lost while you cannot trade.

  • Fire and perils including flood and storm
  • Burglary and theft, with stock and contents
  • Domestic package for buildings, contents and staff
  • All risks for portable high-value items
  • Business interruption and rent loss

Group and corporate

Group medical, group life, WIBA and pension

Employee benefit programmes designed, placed and administered end to end, so your statutory duties are met and your staff understand what they have.

  • Group medical with staff and dependant tiers
  • Group life as a multiple of salary
  • WIBA cover under the Work Injury Benefits Act
  • Group personal accident and last expense
  • Pension and retirement arrangements

Executive and private clients
