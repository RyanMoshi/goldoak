Privacy notice

How GoldOak Insurance Agency collects and uses personal data, in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019.

This is a working draft. Have it reviewed against your actual data practices and your ODPC registration before publishing.

Who we are

GoldOak Insurance Agency Limited is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@goldoak.co.ke.

What we collect

Identification and contact details; details of the assets, vehicles, property or people you want covered; and, where relevant to a medical or life placement, health information. For business clients we also collect company registration details.

Why we collect it

To assess your requirements, obtain quotations from insurers, place and service cover, handle claims, and meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including anti-money-laundering checks.

Who we share it with

Insurers and, where relevant, their appointed assessors and service providers, for the purposes above. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

For as long as you remain a client, and afterwards for the period required by law and by insurers for claims that may still arise.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the personal data we hold, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it, and object to certain processing. Write to info@goldoak.co.ke. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
