About GoldOak

A licensed insurance agency, trading since 2020, working with several insurers so our clients get a real comparison rather than one company's price.

Who we are

GoldOak Insurance Agency is a licensed insurance intermediary in Kenya, regulated by the Insurance Regulatory Authority. We partner with leading insurers to deliver reliable, accessible and client-focused cover across multiple sectors. Since 2020 we have prioritised clarity, integrity and excellence in service delivery.

We are an agency, not a single-company agent. Because we hold appointments with several insurers, we can take one risk to more than one market, compare what comes back, and recommend on merit. Our remuneration is commission paid by the insurer, which means our advice costs you nothing at the point of purchase.

The oak in our name is deliberate. It is slow-growing, deep-rooted and it does not move in a storm.

Vision and mission

Vision

To be the most trusted insurance agency in Kenya, known for integrity, service excellence and client-first solutions.

Mission

To equip clients with comprehensive, affordable and tailor-made insurance solutions that meet their specific needs.

The test we apply. Before any recommendation leaves our desk we ask one question: if this client suffers the loss they are insuring against, will this policy actually respond? If the honest answer is no, we say so and fix it before the cover is placed.

What we stand on

Customer centricity

Your needs decide the recommendation. No advice without a documented needs assessment.

Integrity

Exclusions, limits and excesses disclosed in writing, before you sign.

Solution-driven

We compare the market rather than presenting a single insurer's terms.

Value for money

Price weighed against the cover it actually buys, never in isolation.

Ongoing support

A named contact, and a renewal review 30 days before expiry.

Accountability

Published service standards, and a complaints route that we tell you how to use.

The insurers we work with

We hold appointments with a broad panel so that terms are genuinely competitive and capacity is never the constraint on your cover.

We choose where to place a risk on financial strength, claims record and policy wording — and negotiate on price last, always against the cover it buys.

Dealing with us safely

  • We are licensed by the Insurance Regulatory Authority, and our licence is renewed annually. Ask to see it and we will show you.
  • Premiums are remitted to the insurer as the law requires and are never applied to agency expenses. Always insist on a receipt.
  • Never pay a premium to a personal account. Pay only to official agency or insurer accounts.
  • We handle personal, health and financial data under the Data Protection Act, 2019.
