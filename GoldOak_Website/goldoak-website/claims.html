Claims

A claim is the moment insurance is judged. Call us on +254 729 911 311 as soon as you can.

  1. Notify

    Tell us as soon as you become aware of a loss. Late notification is one of the most common reasons otherwise good claims fail.

  2. Document

    We help assemble the documentation and evidence, and tell you precisely which forms and proofs the insurer will require.

  3. Engage

    We register the claim with the insurer and engage appointed assessors promptly — and chase them when they go quiet.

  4. Update

    We provide regular updates throughout, including when there is no news to give.

  5. Resolve

    We follow through until your compensation is disbursed, and we question a decision where we believe it is wrong.

The first 24 hours after a loss

What you do immediately often decides whether a claim is paid in full.

  • Make people safe first. Injuries and danger come before paperwork.
  • Call us on +254 729 911 311 so we can register the claim and guide you.
  • Report to the police where the loss involves theft, fire, injury or an accident.
  • Photograph everything: the scene, the damage, plates and documents.
  • Do not admit liability or settle privately with a third party.
  • Keep the damaged items. Insurers and assessors may need to inspect them.
  • Do not start repairs until the assessor has been, unless we tell you otherwise.

What we need from you

  • The claim form, fully completed and signed
  • Supporting documents: invoices, valuations, medical or police reports
  • Premium payments current — an unpaid policy may not respond
  • Notice of anything that changes the risk, before it changes
