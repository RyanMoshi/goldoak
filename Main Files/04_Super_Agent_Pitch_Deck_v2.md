## Slide 2 — The problem

**Good insurance advice is buried under four hours of admin.**

To place one SME client properly, an adviser today:
- Re-keys the same client data into 4–6 emails, forms and spreadsheets
- Waits a day or more for insurer replies in six different formats
- Rebuilds the comparison in Excel, then a proposal in Word
- Tracks renewals in a diary and claims in WhatsApp threads

Result: exclusions discovered at claim time, renewals that lapse, and clients who never know where their claim is.

> *Note:* Use one real GoldOak placement as the story — count the emails.

---

## Slide 3 — Why it stays broken

**The tools are built for quoting. The job is advising.**

- Insurer portals: one insurer at a time
- Quote platforms (e.g. Lami): fast quotes, but no needs assessment, no reasoning, no service record
- Generic CRMs: no insurance objects, no comparison, no renewals
- Email + Excel + WhatsApp: free, familiar, undocumented

> *Note:* Name Lami on purpose. Investors who know the market will respect it; those who don't will learn you do.

## Slide 4 — The solution

**One workstation from conversation to claim.**

Fact-find → Risk profile → Standard request to any insurer → Normalised comparison → Reasoned recommendation → Branded proposal → Serviced policy → Automatic renewals → Tracked claim

Works with every insurer from day one — email and document capture now, API when they are ready.

> *Note:* The line "works with every insurer from day one" is the answer to "how will you get integrations?"
