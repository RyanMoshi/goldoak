# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

GoldOak is being built as an **insurance solutions business rather than a policy-selling shop**.

The fundamental idea is simple:

> **Understand the client's risk first. The policy comes after.**

GoldOak's role is to understand what an individual, SME or corporate organisation actually stands to lose, identify the exposures behind that risk, develop an appropriate insurance programme, source suitable terms from a panel of insurers, compare those options, assist the client in selecting and implementing the solution, and remain involved throughout the policy lifecycle.

The relationship does not end when a policy is issued.

GoldOak remains involved through:

**Risk assessment → Recommendation → Market comparison → Placement → Onboarding → Servicing → Claims → Review → Renewal**

This creates a fundamentally different proposition from an intermediary whose primary function is simply to obtain a quotation.

GoldOak's long-term ambition is to combine this client-first insurance model with technology through **Super Agent**, a cloud-based insurance-agent workstation designed to modernise how Kenyan insurance intermediaries obtain quotations, compare insurers, manage clients and produce professional proposals.

The physical agency and the technology platform therefore reinforce one another:

**GoldOak understands the insurance problem.**

**Super Agent improves the infrastructure through which insurance professionals solve it.**

# 2. THE FOUR OPERATING PRINCIPLES

## 2.1 Risk before product

No recommendation should be made before the exposure is understood and documented.

A quotation request without a proper understanding of the client's risk is incomplete work.

We ask:

* What does the client own?
* What does the client do?
* Who depends on the client?
* What could go wrong?
* How likely is it?
* What would the financial consequence be?
* What is already insured?
* What remains uninsured?

---

## 2.2 Appropriate over cheap

The objective is not automatically the cheapest premium.

The objective is the most appropriate solution.

We consider:

* Scope of cover
* Limits
* Benefits
* Exclusions
* Excesses
* Conditions and warranties
* Premium
* Insurer financial strength
* Claims performance
* Service quality

A cheaper policy that does not adequately respond to the client's exposure is not necessarily better value.

---

## 2.3 Options, not assumptions

Where the market permits, GoldOak provides multiple options and compares them on equivalent terms.

The client should be able to see:

* What each insurer is offering
* What is covered
* What is excluded
* Limits
* Excesses
* Conditions
* Premium
* Material differences

We do not assume which insurer should win.

---

## 2.4 The relationship outlives the policy

Issuing the policy is the middle of the job, not the end.

GoldOak remains involved through:

* Endorsements
* Changes
* Questions
* Certificates
* Premium reminders
* Claims
* Renewals
* Annual reviews
* Changes in the client's risk

A client who only hears from GoldOak when their policy is about to expire is a client the agency is slowly losing.

# 4. CRITICAL REGULATORY STRUCTURE

There is one foundational issue that must be settled before GoldOak's public positioning, contracts and marketing materials are finalised.

The operating model described throughout this document has characteristics associated with **broker behaviour** because it:

* Represents the client's interests.
* Approaches multiple insurers.
* Compares competing insurance terms.
* Recommends the most suitable option.

However, the business has also been described as an **insurance agency**.

In Kenya, the regulatory distinction between an insurance agent and insurance broker matters.

It affects:

* Licensing
* Representation
* Insurer relationships
* Remuneration
* Disclosure
* Professional indemnity requirements
* Client communications
* What GoldOak can legally promise clients

Therefore:

> **GoldOak must confirm its current IRA licence category and ensure the operating model, public language and contractual arrangements match that licence.**

This is a business-critical decision, not merely a wording issue.

The final company profile should only use terms such as **independent**, **broker**, **agency**, **client representative**, or similar descriptions after the regulatory position has been confirmed.

Insurance Regulatory Authority:

[Insurance Regulatory Authority of Kenya](https://www.ira.go.ke/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

* Business property
* Motor
* Employees
* Medical
* WIBA
* Group life
* Liability
* Professional indemnity
* Equipment
* Business interruption
* Owner/family insurance

Therefore, GoldOak should measure SME performance using:

> **Covers per client relationship**

rather than simply:

> Policies sold.

If the average SME relationship contains only one policy, GoldOak risks becoming a policy shop.

# 46. THE PROBLEM

Insurance agents can spend significant amounts of time manually requesting quotations.

The current workflow can involve:

**Client enquiry**

↓

**Collect information**

↓

**Email insurers**

↓

**Call insurer contacts**

↓

**Wait for responses**

↓

**Receive different formats**

↓

**Enter data into Excel**

↓

**Compare manually**

↓

**Prepare quotation**

↓

**Send to client**

This can consume hours of an intermediary's time.

The consequences include:

* Slow response times
* Human errors
* Inconsistent comparisons
* Lost leads
* Lower daily client capacity
* Poor client experience
* Administrative overhead

---

# 47. THE SUPER AGENT SOLUTION

Super Agent is designed to centralise the process.

The proposed system:

1. Captures client information.
2. Identifies the relevant insurance product.
3. Connects to insurer product catalogues/rating systems.
4. Obtains quotations.
5. Compares options.
6. Produces side-by-side results.
7. Generates branded client-ready quotations.
8. Stores the client record.
9. Tracks policies and renewals.
10. Supports CRM functionality.

The intended experience is:

> **From client enquiry to professional comparison in minutes rather than hours.**

The stated product ambition is to deliver real-time or near-real-time comparison, potentially producing results in under sixty seconds where integrations and insurer systems support that speed.
