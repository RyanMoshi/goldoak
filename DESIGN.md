## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, calm, and sovereign visual language tailored for the next generation of African insurance distribution and financial advisory. Rooted initially in the East African economic corridor, it merges the quiet authority of private wealth management with the operational precision of an enterprise operating system.

### Philosophy & Visual Movement
The aesthetic aligns with **Institutional Modernism** and **Tactile Financial Editorial**:
- **Dignified Restraint:** Eliminates cartoonish tech tropes, hyper-saturated neon accents, and decorative micro-interactions. Every visual marker signifies permanence, fiduciary duty, and solvency.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Heavy reliance on tabular clarity, razor-sharp ledger lines, and strict baseline alignment. Data density is treated as a professional affordance rather than clutter.
- **African Heritage Reinterpreted:** Grounded in organic, terrestrial tones—rich Kenyan upland forest greens, archival warm cream paper stock, and raw Savannah ochre gold. It honors local fiscal culture while delivering international enterprise utility.

## Colors

The palette balances the physical weight of leather-bound policy ledgers and certified paper notes with clean digital contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas (`#F7F4EC`):** A warm, tactile cream substrate that eliminates eye fatigue during all-day field and terminal usage, differentiating the interface from clinical SaaS whites.
- **Forest Slate (`#073423`):** The primary brand anchor. Used for primary navigation rails, top-level headers, command interfaces, and primary button hover states. Reflects trust, institutional grounding, and deep reserve backing.
- **Savannah Gold (`#C28D38`):** The operational accent. Reserved strictly for primary call-to-actions, active pipeline stages, active filter pills, and copilot interaction highlights.
- **Ink Core (`#16211B`) & Muted Ink (`#5D6B63`):** High-legibility typographic tokens calculated for strict WCAG AAA contrast against cream and pure white card surfaces.
- **Emerald Reserve (`#1F7A4D`):** Applied to active underwriting statuses, paid commission ledgers, M-Pesa settlement confirmation badges, and positive growth indicators.
- **Crimson & Ochre (`#B3261E` / `#C47C1B`):** System warnings and lapse notices calibrated to alert without inducing panic.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances literary gravitas with computational precision.

### Typographic Roles
- **Editorial Voice (Merriweather):** Reserved for portfolio valuations, executive summaries, client names on policy dockets, and top-tier metrics. It projects the established trust of legal contracts and physical registries.
- **Ergonomic Workspace (Karla):** The workhorse sans-serif for inputs, table content, workflow steps, and navigational structures. Selected for its compact proportions and unpretentious legibility in dense forms.
- **Ledger Monospace (JetBrains Mono):** Mandatory for all financial and identity payloads: Kenyan Shillings (`KES 1,240,500.00`), Underwriting Slip IDs, National ID numbers, KRA PIN codes, Policy Endorsements, and premium loss ratios. Numbers must line up tabularly without jitter.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine uses a high-density, mathematical 4px/8px rhythm. Screens are built to convey maximum context with minimal vertical displacement, respecting the speed required by field agents toggling between underwriting guidelines, CRM pipelines, and payment processing.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fixed 240px collapsable structural navigation rail, followed by an asymmetrical 12-column workspace (16px gutters). Side-docked AI copilot drawers occupy a rigid 360px utility zone.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Split-view master-detail layouts. Left pane lists leads or policies; right pane loads inspection logs and ledger sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column vertical stream, bottom sheet sheets replace slide-out utility drawers, horizontal scroll ledgers for data tables with pinned freeze columns for client name and premium totals.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects dramatic multi-layered drop shadows in favor of **structural containment and low-contrast borders**.

### Boundary-Defined Stacking
- **Flat Surface Tier:** Primary surfaces are strictly demarcated using 1px solid borders (`#E8E3D5`) on top of the `#F7F4EC` cream canvas.
- **Direct Elevation:** Floating context elements (dropdown menus, popover risk scorecards, modal KYC verification sheets) use a disciplined, hyper-diffused ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(7, 52, 35, 0.08), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(7, 52, 35, 0.04);`
- **Tonal Contrast:** Active sidebar items and highlighted metric panels do not elevate via Z-axis; they invert their background (Forest `#073423` with white and gold typography) to declare presence.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is tailored specifically per component utility rather than a blanket corner radius:

- **Interactive Controls (6px):** Buttons, inputs, search dropdown triggers, and select selectors utilize a sharp-soft 6px corner radius. This prevents controls from feeling like consumer toys while softening physical tap targets.
- **Cards and Containers (10px):** Client profile sheets, policy summary cards, and copilot insight boxes utilize a stable 10px boundary.
- **Data Grids & Financial Ledgers (0px):** Tabular data displays, policy schedule tables, and audit logs must remain completely square-cornered (`border-radius: 0px`). Edges connect cleanly with hairline dividers, reinforcing the feeling of an immutable legal register.

## Components

### Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#C28D38` (Gold), Text `#FFFFFF` or deep Ink `#073423`, border-radius 6px, font-weight 700. Padding: 10px 18px. Active/hover state transitions to `#A8752B`.
- **Institutional Primary:** Background `#073423` (Forest), Text `#FFFFFF`, 6px radius. Used for official binding, document generation, and policy finalization.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#E8E3D5` border, Text `#16211B`. Hover state applies `#FFFFFF` background with border `#5D6B63`.

### Policy & Financial Data Tables
- **Structure:** `0px` radius, full-bleed or bordered within an open card container.
- **Header Row:** Background `#F2EFE5`, text `#5D6B63`, all caps, 11px JetBrains Mono/Karla, tracking 0.08em. Border bottom 1px solid `#D8D2C2`.
- **Cell Rows:** Height 44px for high density. Currency data (KES) right-aligned in JetBrains Mono (`13px`). Statuses left-aligned. Alternating row fills are prohibited; separation occurs via 1px horizontal dividers (`#F0ECE1`).

### Form Controls & Underwriting Inputs
- **Default Input:** 1px border `#E8E3D5`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#16211B`, height 40px, padding 0 12px, radius 6px. Placeholder text `#8A968F`.
- **Active Focus:** Border transitions to `#073423` with a 1px matching ring outline; zero fuzzy glow.
- **Prefix / Suffix Elements:** Static currency markers (`KES`) or identity selectors (`ID / PIN`) set in `#5D6B63` within an embedded border enclosure.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Policy Active:** Background `rgba(31, 122, 77, 0.12)`, text `#1F7A4D`, border 1px solid `rgba(31, 122, 77, 0.25)`, radius 4px, font 11px JetBrains Mono.
- **Pending Underwriter:** Background `rgba(196, 124, 27, 0.12)`, text `#C47C1B`, border 1px solid `rgba(196, 124, 27, 0.25)`.
- **Lapsed / Claim Rejected:** Background `rgba(179, 38, 30, 0.08)`, text `#B3261E`, border 1px solid `rgba(179, 38, 30, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Summary Panels
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E8E3D5`, radius 10px, interior padding 20px. 
- **Card Headers:** Metric labels set in uppercase `label-caps` (`#5D6B63`), accompanied by figures rendered in `Merriweather` or `data-mono-lg`.

### AI Copilot Workspace
- **Integration:** Slide-out or split-screen rail surfaced in clean white with a dedicated `#073423` header stripe.
- **Intelligence Markers:** AI suggestions use subtle left-accent borders in `#C28D38` (Savannah Gold) with background `#FCFAF7`, avoiding generic sparkles or neon tech icons in favor of structured audit trail points.
